- R-DESIGN-QUALITY: Assess the design system, composition, alignment, contrast, distribution, placement and sizing, responsiveness, fluid component and route animation and transitions, and reduced motion; exercise per-element hover where applicable, press or tap, keyboard focus and disabled states where relevant. Apply platform conventions and preserve creative latitude.
- R-DESIGN-AUDIT: For existing interfaces, inspect the original render and identify concrete components and states, including what works. Use that evidence and the brief to choose no change when nothing material needs correction, targeted polish for local problems, or redesign when direction is in scope and problems are systemic or when the brief explicitly asks for a new direction. Preserve working workflows, meaningful content and deliberate expressive choices unless the brief authorizes changing them. When the original cannot be rendered, name that limit and do not claim a visual finding from code alone.
- R-DESIGN-SIGNATURE: When the product and platform benefit, put expressive effort into one or two meaningful focal points or a recurring product-specific detail, keeping surrounding controls subordinate where useful. This is a creative choice, never a requirement to add ornament, strip intentional ornament, or impose a visual style.
- R-DESIGN-DEPENDENCIES: Sequence corrections by what they depend on; settle copy that drives typography and layout before fitting them, and tune motion after the layout and states are stable. Compare the revised render and relevant states against the original when possible; use the checks the work warrants.
